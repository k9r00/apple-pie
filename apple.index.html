🍎apple pie recipe🍏

Ingredients
Here's a small apple pie recipe (serves 2 to 4)

For the filling

2 medium apples, peeled and sliced
1 tsp cinnamon
2 tbsp sugar
1 tsp cinnamon
1 tsp lemon juice

For the crust

1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
¼ tsp salt
4 tbsp cold butter
cubed 2 to 3 tbsp cold water

Instructions

Make the dough: Mix flour and salt. Rub in the cold butter until crumbly. Add cold water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until a dough forms. Chill for 15 minutes. Prepare the filling: Combine apples, sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice in a bowl. Assemble: Heat oven to 190°C (375°F). Roll out two-thirds of the dough and line a small pie dish (about 15 cm / 6 in). Add the apple filling. Roll out the remaining dough and place it on top. Seal and crimp the edges. Cut a few small vents in the top. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden brown. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.

Enjoy warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or a little whipped cream! 🍎🥧
